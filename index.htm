The Trail Church promise

Every need has a visible path.

01 · REQUEST

Preacher submits

An exact item is requested with its ministry purpose and quantity.

02 · REVIEW

Need is verified

Trail Church checks the product, cost, delivery details, and need.

03 · FUND

People give openly

Supporters appear under chosen usernames on the funding ledger.

04 · FULFILL

Purchase is documented

A receipt and delivery update finish the public record.

Nothing disappears

Public proof, without exposing private information.

Every fulfilled need receives a public record of what was purchased, its final cost, and its delivery status. Personal addresses and payment information remain private.

If a retailer price or availability changes, the need moves to review. Funds are never silently redirected.
