The Trail Church promise

Every need has a visible path.

01 · REQUEST

Preacher submits

A preacher requests an exact item and explains its ministry purpose.

02 · REVIEW

Need is verified

Trail Church reviews the product, cost, quantity, and delivery details.

03 · FUND

People give openly

Contributions appear under chosen usernames on the public funding ledger.

04 · FULFILL

Purchase is documented

Staff place the order, then publish a redacted receipt and delivery update.

Nothing disappears

Public proof, without exposing private information.

Every fulfilled need receives a simple public record: what was purchased, the final cost, the date, and the delivery status. Personal delivery and payment details remain private.

When an item sells out or costs more than expected, the need moves to review. Funds are never silently redirected.
